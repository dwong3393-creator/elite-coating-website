About Elite Coating

An ISO-certified industrial coating company in Sungai Petani, Kedah, serving manufacturers who need consistent, repeatable finishes on metal and plastic components.

Elite Coating Sdn. Bhd. specialises in surface coating and painting for metal and plastic parts, serving a wide range of industries including automotive, electronics and lighting.

Our coating line brings pre-treatment, spray booths and curing ovens together, so each part moves through every stage in one place. Our work ranges from die-cast housings and sheet-metal enclosures to mirror-finish lamp reflectors, with tampo and silkscreen printing for markings.

We are certified to ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 14001:2015, and have received awards from customers for quality, delivery and service.

Caring for the environment

Coating involves chemicals, waste water and energy, so we manage them carefully. Looking after the environment is part of how Elite Coating runs every day, and our environmental management system is certified to ISO 14001:2015.

Our plant treats its waste water on site, and our laboratory checks process chemicals so they stay within standard. Getting each job right the first time protects the quality of your parts and avoids unnecessary rework and waste. See our certifications

Our efforts focus on

  • Minimising waste throughout the coating process
  • Preventing pollution from our processes
  • Meeting applicable environmental laws and regulations
  • Improving our environmental performance over time
