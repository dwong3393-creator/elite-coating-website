Privacy Notice

How Elite Coating Sdn. Bhd. looks after the personal information you give us.

Elite Coating Sdn. Bhd. (Company No. 200301024073 (626493-K)) runs this website. This notice explains how we handle your personal data under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

What we collect

When you send us an enquiry, we collect what you enter in the form: your name, company, email address, phone number, details of the parts you want coated, and any message or files you add. Your name and email address are needed so that we can reply.

Why we use it

We use your details to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quotation and talk to you about your order. We do not use them for anything else without asking you first.

Who sees it

Your enquiry goes to our staff by email. We share personal data only where it is needed to deal with your enquiry, or where the law requires it.

This website

This website does not use advertising or analytics tools, and its own pages do not set cookies. Your choice of light or dark theme is saved in your browser and stays on your device. Pages with a map load Google Maps, and Google may collect information about your visit under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and to keep our business records. After that we delete them.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to stop using it. Contact us using the details below and we will reply as the PDPA requires.
